# Collections Model Monitoring Standard

## Scope
This standard applies to models used to prioritize delinquent accounts or select collection treatment.

## Required monitoring
Review PSI, rank-ordering performance, cure rate, roll rate, contactability, treatment mix, promise-to-pay outcomes, and performance by delinquency bucket.

## Risk considerations
Performance must be checked by delinquency stage and treatment strategy because aggregate results can hide deterioration in high-risk collections segments.

## Escalation
Any RED metric requires escalation. Two or more AMBER metrics require investigation. Material changes in collection strategy must be assessed as a possible driver before concluding model deterioration.

## Governance
Automated recommendations must not independently change customer treatment strategies without authorized human approval.
