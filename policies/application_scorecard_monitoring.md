# Application Scorecard Monitoring Standard

## Scope
This standard applies to application probability-of-default scorecards used for retail underwriting decisions.

## Required monitoring
Review monthly population stability, AUC/Gini, bad rate, approval rate, missing values, score distribution, channel mix, and New-to-Credit performance.

## Population stability
Overall PSI must be supplemented by segment PSI for acquisition channel, geography, New-to-Credit status, and major policy segments. A RED overall PSI should not automatically be interpreted as model deterioration; the analyst must identify which segments contributed to the shift.

## Escalation
Apply the enterprise Monitoring Policy and Escalation Procedure. Any RED metric requires escalation and documented investigation.

## Commentary expectation
Monitoring commentary should distinguish model-performance change from population-mix, underwriting-policy, and acquisition-channel changes.
