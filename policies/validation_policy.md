# Model Validation Policy

## Purpose
Independent validation must assess whether a model remains fit for its approved use and whether monitoring evidence supports continued reliance on the model.

## Validation triggers
Targeted revalidation is required when there is sustained RED performance deterioration, a material change in model design or data, a significant change in use, repeated unresolved monitoring issues, or a material control failure.

## Monitoring evidence
Validators must review current and historical performance, critical segments, data-quality exceptions, threshold breaches, business changes, overrides, known limitations, and previous remediation items.

## Challenge expectations
Validation must independently challenge the monitoring methodology, threshold rationale, root-cause conclusions, and management actions. Aggregate performance alone is not sufficient where critical segments show material deterioration.

## Closure
A validation issue may be closed only when evidence demonstrates that the root cause has been addressed or the residual risk has been formally accepted by the appropriate governance authority.
