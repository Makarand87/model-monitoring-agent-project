# Behaviour Scorecard Monitoring Standard

## Scope
This standard applies to behavioural risk models used for existing-customer account management.

## Required monitoring
Review PSI, AUC/Gini, delinquency migration, utilization, payment behaviour, limit changes, missing values, and performance by account-age and risk-band segments.

## Stability interpretation
A material PSI shift must be investigated for changes in utilization, payment patterns, account tenure, hardship programmes, and portfolio strategy. Segment analysis is required before attributing a shift to model deterioration.

## Escalation
Any RED metric is escalated under the enterprise Monitoring Policy. Two or more AMBER metrics require documented investigation even when no individual metric is RED.

## Special consideration
Rapid changes in repayment behaviour following policy or macroeconomic changes must be explicitly documented in monitoring commentary.
